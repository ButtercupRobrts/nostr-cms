  <action>
    CAUTION: `save()` itself calls `s.mu.Lock()` — calling `s.save()` synchronously inside `ListPending` (which holds the write lock) DEADLOCKS. Correct fix: split into `saveLocked()` (marshals s.posts + writes file, assumes caller holds lock) and `save()` (locks, calls saveLocked). Alternative acceptable fix: a single-writer queue — but the saveLocked split is smaller.
  </action>

  <acceptance_criteria>

    - No goroutine persists state; save is synchronous and non-deadlocking
  </acceptance_criteria>
